WHAT WE DO

INTERIOR DESIGN

Comprehensive interior architectural concepts developed from spatial intention, materiality, and light to create enduring sanctuaries.

SPATIAL CONVERSATION • TRAVERTINE, TIMBER & LIGHT RESIDENTIAL
“From concept to character, we create interiors that balance material, light and form with the way you live.”

WHAT IT INCLUDES

Design Scope

Every stage is shaped with precision to ensure architectural clarity, harmonious textures, and cohesive living.

  • 01

    Spatial Concept & Narrative

    Formulating a cohesive architectural vision, volume hierarchy, and design character tailored to your daily rituals.

  • 02

    Materiality & Finishes Curation

    Selecting natural stones, fluted timbers, warm lime-washes, micro-cements, and curated tactile surfaces.

  • 03

    Custom Joinery & Millwork Detailing

    Designing bespoke cabinetry, architectural shelving, wall paneling, and integrated storage solutions.

  • 04

    Lighting Architecture & Fixtures

    Layering ambient, architectural, and accent illumination to create atmosphere, shadow play, and warmth.

  • 05

    Technical Drawing Documentation

    Producing precise elevations, reflected ceiling plans, joinery details, and full finish schedules.
