WHAT IT INCLUDES

Styling Scope

Every fixture, surface ornament, and textile is thoughtfully chosen to elevate comfort and sensory depth.

  • 01

    Loose Furniture & Upholstery Selection

    Curating sculptural armchairs, refined dining tables, custom sofas, and organic occasional pieces.

  • 02

    Decorative & Ambient Lighting

    Sourcing handcrafted ceramic lamps, understated floor fixtures, and statement pendants for soft warmth.

  • 03

    Fine Art & Gallery Placement

    Advising on art acquisitions, custom archival framing, focal wall curation, and sculptural display.

  • 04

    Textiles, Rugs & Tactile Layering

    Selecting hand-knotted wool rugs, washed Belgian linens, textured bouclés, and raw silk window drapery.

  • 05

    On-Site Staging & Object Styling

    Curating artisan pottery, vintage vessels, books, and botanicals with intentional composition.

OUR METHOD

The Styling Process

01

Aesthetic Direction

Defining the tactile palette, color nuances, and collected character desired for the home.

02

Sourcing & Selection

Curating pieces from master artisans, design galleries, specialized ateliers, and vintage sources.

03

Composition & Balance

Harmonizing proportion scales, light reflections, fabric weights, and tactile surface pairings.

04

On-Site Installation

Meticulously placing each object, dressing furnishings, and delivering a warm, complete living ambiance.
