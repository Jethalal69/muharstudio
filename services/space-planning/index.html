MUHAR STUDIO — CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
WHAT IT INCLUDES

Planning Scope

Every layout is meticulously analyzed to maximize livability, sightline aesthetics, and spatial volume.

  • 01

    Circulation & Movement Analysis

    Mapping natural circulation pathways and transitional sightlines to ensure effortless movement through every room.

  • 02

    Functional Zoning & Volume Allocation

    Defining dedicated functional zones—gathering, dining, rest, and utility—without compromising openness or light.

  • 03

    Furniture Scale & Proportion Balance

    Calibrating piece dimensions, focal points, and clearance ratios to create balanced, uncrowded living environments.

  • 04

    Natural Light & Sightline Optimization

    Aligning architectural apertures and room configurations to maximize natural daylight and serene visual depth.

  • 05

    Dimensional Architectural Floorplans

    Delivering scaled 2D spatial layouts with complete dimensional callouts, furniture footprints, and clearance guides.

OUR METHOD

The Planning Process

01

Spatial Assessment

Evaluating architectural volume, structural boundaries, entry transitions, and natural light sources.

02

Flow & Zoning Strategy

Formulating functional quadrants, primary movement arteries, and intuitive spatial divisions.

03

Layout Iterations

Testing multiple furniture arrangements, scale configurations, and spatial clearances for optimal balance.

04

Master Blueprint

Delivering fully finalized architectural layout schematics ready for execution or design integration.
