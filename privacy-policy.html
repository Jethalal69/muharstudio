Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · MUHAR STUDIO (Noida, India)

1. Introduction & Scope

MUHAR STUDIO (“we”, “our”, or “studio”) is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our clients, prospective patrons, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, and safeguard personal information submitted through our website (muharstudio.com) and during design consultations.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our studio via our consultation or contact forms, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, phone number.
  • Project Specifications: Project type (Residential, Commercial, Space Planning, Execution, Décor), approximate budget range, property location, timeline, and spatial requirements.
  • Technical Data: Standard server logs, IP addresses, and browser information strictly for security and performance optimization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your submitted details exclusively to:

  • Review project feasibility and schedule architectural design consultations.
  • Deliver bespoke design proposals, execution schedules, and studio correspondence.
  • Ensure website integrity, accessibility, and security compliance.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties or marketing brokers.

4. Confidentiality & Architectural Data

Floor plans, CAD drawings, photography of private residences, and client financial details shared during project execution are treated under strict professional confidentiality. Portfolio showcases on this website only display completed projects with client consent.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website operates on minimal, privacy-first technical infrastructure. We do not employ third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels. Any future analytics integration will operate under anonymous measurement standards.

6. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or to request the deletion of your consultation inquiry details, please contact us at:
